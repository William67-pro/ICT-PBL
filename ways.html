How Can We Help

Way to reduce air pollution

  • Use public transport, carpool, cycle, or walk.
  • Reduce use of coal and diesel generators.
  • Do not burn garbage, leaves, or plastic.
  • Use cleaner fuels and technologies.

Way to reduce water pollution

  • Do not throw trash, oil, paint, or chemicals into drains, rivers, or lakes.
  • Use environmentally friendly cleaning products.
  • Reduce plastic use and recycle whenever possible.
  • Do not pour cooking oil or chemicals down sinks.

Way to reduce land or soil pollution

  • Reduce unnecessary waste and packaging.
  • Throw garbage in designated bins.
  • Separate biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste.
  • Plant more trees

Way to reduce plastic pollution

  • Separate plastic waste from other garbage.
  • Follow local recycling rules.
  • Buy products with less packaging.
  • Use paper, glass, or metal alternatives when possible.
